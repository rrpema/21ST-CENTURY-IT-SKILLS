</h2>

</div>

<div align="justify">

For this activity, I created a promotional poster for a free coffee event. The goal of this design was to create a simple, clean, and visually appealing poster that communicates the important details immediately without overwhelming the viewer.

The poster uses a coffee-themed background to establish the main concept while creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The coffee texture serves as the visual foundation, allowing the important elements such as the title, coffee cup illustration, logo, location, and event details to stand out.

</div>

<div align="center">

<h3>
𝑫𝒆𝒔𝒊𝒈𝒏 𝑨𝒑𝒑𝒓𝒐𝒂𝒄𝒉
</h3>

</div>

<div align="justify">

I prefer designs that are simple and direct to the point. Instead of adding excessive decorations and unnecessary elements, I focused on creating a layout that is easy to understand and comfortable to view.

A simple design allows the audience to quickly recognize the purpose of the poster and identify the important information without difficulty.

The poster applies the following design principles:

★ **Visual Hierarchy** – The title "FREE COFFEE" is placed prominently to immediately capture attention, followed by supporting details such as the location and event information.

★ **Contrast** – The use of white text and elements against the darker coffee background improves readability and allows important information to stand out.

★ **Balance** – The placement of the logo, coffee cup illustration, text, and other elements creates a balanced composition without making the poster feel crowded.

★ **Consistency** – The colors, typography, and overall theme were kept consistent to maintain a clean and cohesive appearance.

</div>

<div align="center">

<h3>
𝑪𝒓𝒆𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒗𝒆 𝑷𝒓𝒐𝒄𝒆𝒔𝒔
</h3>

</div>

<div align="justify">

During the creation process, I focused on limiting unnecessary elements and prioritizing the main message of the poster. I chose a minimalist approach because overly designed posters can sometimes make information harder to process.

By maintaining enough spacing and using only essential details, the poster becomes more visually comfortable, easier to understand, and more effective in delivering its message.
