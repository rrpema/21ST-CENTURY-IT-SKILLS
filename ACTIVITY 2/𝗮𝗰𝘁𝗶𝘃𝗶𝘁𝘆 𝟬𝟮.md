<h3>
𝑷𝒆𝒓𝒔𝒐𝒏𝒂𝒍 𝑩𝒓𝒂𝒏𝒅𝒊𝒏𝒈
</h3>
<br>

</div>

<div align="justify">

For this activity, I created my own personal branding concept that reflects my personality, preferences, and creative style. The goal of this design was to develop a visual identity that represents who I am while maintaining a balance between professionalism, creativity, and individuality.

I chose this design direction mainly because I appreciate the interplay between **muted colors and solid backgrounds**. This combination creates a formal yet playful appearance, allowing the overall branding to feel organized, calm, and visually appealing while still expressing personality.

The branding incorporates a mixture of different typography styles, combining expressive and unique fonts with more traditional serif fonts. This contrast represents the balance between creativity and professionalism, creating a design that feels both distinctive and easy to recognize.

</div>

<div align="center">

<h3>
𝑫𝒆𝒔𝒊𝒈𝒏 𝑨𝒑𝒑𝒓𝒐𝒂𝒄𝒉
</h3>

</div>

<div align="justify">

The color palette was carefully selected to create a cohesive visual identity. The use of muted tones allows the design to appear sophisticated and approachable, while the darker solid backgrounds provide contrast and make the visual elements stand out.

For typography, I combined playful display fonts with clean and readable typefaces. The expressive fonts add character and creativity, while the serif fonts maintain balance and professionalism. This combination helps communicate a personality that is both imaginative and refined.

</div>

<div align="center">

<h3>
𝑫𝒆𝒔𝒊𝒈𝒏 𝑷𝒓𝒊𝒏𝒄𝒊𝒑𝒍𝒆𝒔 𝑨𝒑𝒑𝒍𝒊𝒆𝒅
</h3>

</div>

<div align="justify">

★ **Contrast** – The combination of different colors, backgrounds, and typography styles creates emphasis and improves visual impact.

★ **Balance** – The placement of colors, shapes, and text creates a harmonious composition that avoids overwhelming the viewer.

★ **Consistency** – Repeated use of the chosen color palette, typography, and design elements strengthens the overall identity of the personal brand.

★ **Visual Hierarchy** – The use of different font sizes and styles guides attention toward the most important parts of the design.

</div>

<div align="center">

<h3>
𝑪𝒓𝒆𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒗𝒆 𝑷𝒓𝒐𝒄𝒆𝒔𝒔
</h3>

</div>

<div align="justify">

During the creation process, I focused on creating a personal brand that reflects my own aesthetic preferences. Instead of adding excessive elements, I prioritized simplicity and intentional design choices to ensure that every component contributes to the overall identity.

This activity helped me understand that personal branding is not only about creating an attractive design, but also about communicating personality, values, and individuality through visual elements. Through careful selection of colors, typography, and composition, I was able to create a design that represents my personal style while maintaining a professional appearance.
