<h2>
𝑺𝒐𝒄𝒊𝒂𝒍 𝑴𝒆𝒅𝒊𝒂 𝑰𝒏𝒇𝒐𝒈𝒓𝒂𝒑𝒉𝒊𝒄
</h2>

</div>

<div align="justify">

For this activity, I created an infographic focusing on a **Smart Campus Parking Management and Tracking System**. I chose this topic because it reflects a personal experience that I encounter as a student, where finding available parking spaces around the campus can be challenging and time-consuming.

Through this infographic, I wanted to highlight a common campus concern and present a technology-assisted solution that can improve the overall parking experience for students, faculty, and parking attendants. The design explains the existing challenges such as limited parking spaces, high demand, and the need for a more organized system that provides real-time parking information.

The infographic incorporates different visual elements that are directly connected to the topic, including parking icons, vehicles, mobile applications, monitoring dashboards, and location indicators. These elements were intentionally used to make the concept more understandable and relatable while showing how technology can help address everyday campus concerns.

</div>

<div align="center">

<h3>
𝑫𝒆𝒔𝒊𝒈𝒏 𝑨𝒑𝒑𝒓𝒐𝒂𝒄𝒉
</h3>

</div>

<div align="justify">

In creating this infographic, I focused on presenting information in a structured and easy-to-follow manner. Since the topic involves identifying a problem and proposing a technological solution, I organized the design into sections that show the problem, proposed system, process flow, benefits, and key features.

I included visual elements that represent each part of the proposed system, such as checking parking availability, selecting parking spaces, updating availability status, and monitoring parking usage. These illustrations help simplify the concept and allow viewers to understand the system without relying only on text.

The overall design follows a clean and organized approach by using consistent colors, icons, and layouts. The combination of blue and green tones creates a modern technology-based appearance while maintaining a professional and campus-friendly theme.

</div>

<div align="center">

<h3>
𝑫𝒆𝒔𝒊𝒈𝒏 𝑷𝒓𝒊𝒏𝒄𝒊𝒑𝒍𝒆𝒔 𝑨𝒑𝒑𝒍𝒊𝒆𝒅
</h3>

</div>

<div align="justify">

★ **Visual Hierarchy** – The infographic presents information in a logical sequence, beginning with the parking problem, followed by the proposed solution, process, and expected benefits.

★ **Contrast** – The use of different colors, text sizes, and visual elements highlights important information and improves readability.

★ **Balance** – The placement of illustrations, text sections, and diagrams creates a balanced layout that prevents the infographic from appearing overcrowded.

★ **Consistency** – The repeated use of icons, colors, and typography creates a unified design that strengthens the overall message.

</div>

<div align="center">

<h3>
𝑪𝒓𝒆𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒗𝒆 𝑷𝒓𝒐𝒄𝒆𝒔𝒔
</h3>

</div>

<div align="justify">

During the creation process, I focused on transforming a personal experience into a visual representation of a possible solution. Since parking difficulties are something that students commonly experience, I wanted to create an infographic that not only explains the problem but also introduces a practical technology-based approach.

I prioritized clarity and simplicity by selecting only elements that contribute to the main message. Instead of adding unnecessary decorations, I focused on creating a design where every icon, section, and visual component supports the idea of improving campus parking management.

This activity helped me understand how technology and design can work together to communicate real-world problems and solutions. Through proper organization of information and thoughtful visual choices, an infographic can make complex ideas easier to understand and more engaging for the audience.
